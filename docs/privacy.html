Parenting Helper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Parenting Helper ("the app") is made by Pieter Britz, trading as ByteForged Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

The app is designed for parents and legal guardians aged 18 or over. It helps you manage your children's daily routines and rewards.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your device. We never receive it.
  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • You can delete a child, or all data, at any time in the app.

Information you enter

To use the app, you enter:

  • Your children's first names or nicknames, and the avatar you pick for each.
  • Tasks, routines, stars, rewards and reward history.
  • A 4-digit parent PIN. Only a salted, one-way hash of the PIN is stored, never the PIN itself.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We recommend entering only a first name or nickname for each child.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app receives only a confirmation from Google Play that a subscription is active, which it uses to unlock Premium features on your device. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Children's privacy

The app is for parents and guardians, not for children to use on their own. A parent PIN protects rewards, purchases, deletions and settings.

We do not collect personal information from children. Information about your children stays on your device, under your control. This is consistent with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's data provisions of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Your rights and choices

Because your information stays on your device, you control it directly:

  • Access and correct: view and edit your children's details in the app.
  • Delete: delete a child in the Kids tab, or everything in Settings > Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the app and delete its data at any time.

If you live in the EU, UK or another region with data protection laws, you may have further rights, such as complaining to your local data protection authority. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing we can access, export or delete on our side.

Permissions

The app uses Google Play's billing service to offer subscriptions. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security and app sandboxing. Keep your device locked with a screen lock, and choose a parent PIN your children won't guess.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information (for example, if we add optional cloud sync), we will update this policy and ask you to agree to it again in the app before the change applies to you.
